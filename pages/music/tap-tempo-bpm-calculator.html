How to find a song's BPM

Put the song on and tap the big button on every beat: usually the steady pulse you'd nod your head to, where the kick and snare land. After two taps you get a reading, and it settles as you keep tapping. The calculator averages the gaps between your last eight beats, so one early or late tap doesn't throw it off. Stop for two seconds and the next tap starts a new count.

If you'd rather not tap, count beats while watching a clock and enter the count and the seconds in the manual calculator.

The formula

tap tempo: BPM = 60,000 ÷ average gap between taps (ms)
manual: BPM = beats counted × 60 ÷ seconds
half time = BPM ÷ 2   ·   double time = BPM × 2

Tips for an accurate reading

  • Tap at least eight times. Human timing wobbles by a few milliseconds, and averaging more taps smooths that out.
  • Lock onto the snare or kick, not the vocal, which often pushes ahead of or lags behind the beat.
  • Check half and double time. If the reading feels too slow or too fast for the genre, the other one is probably what a DJ or DAW would call it.
  • Watch for tempo changes. Live recordings drift; tap through a full chorus for a fair average, or reset and tap each section.
