How the grind time is worked out

Whether it's an MMO level cap, a battle pass, a skill in RuneScape or a prestige rank, every grind is the same sum: what's left divided by how fast you earn it. The hard part is knowing your real XP per hour, so measure a few normal sessions rather than using your best one.

The formula

XP left = target − current
hours = XP left ÷ (XP per hour × bonus)
days = hours ÷ hours played per day
hours per day for a deadline = hours ÷ days left

Grinding smarter

  • Save it for bonus events. Double XP weekends halve the time; plan big sessions around them.
  • Stack daily rewards. Daily quests and first-win bonuses often beat hours of repetitive farming per minute played.
  • Take breaks. Your XP per hour drops when you're tired; two focused hours usually beat four sluggish ones.
