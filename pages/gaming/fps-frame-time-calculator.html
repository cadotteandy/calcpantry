How FPS and frame time relate

Frames per second counts how many images your PC or console finishes each second. Frame time is the same thing upside down: how long each one takes. Because they're reciprocals, equal FPS gains get smaller and smaller in real time as frame rates climb.

The formula

frame time (ms) = 1000 ÷ FPS
FPS = 1000 ÷ frame time (ms)
refresh interval (ms) = 1000 ÷ refresh rate (Hz)

Getting smoother frames

  • Cap your frame rate. A cap just under your refresh rate, with G-Sync or FreeSync on, gives steady frame times and avoids tearing.
  • Watch the 1% lows. Stutter comes from the slowest frames. Lowering CPU-heavy settings (view distance, crowds, shadows) often helps more than lowering resolution.
  • Don't chase the last few ms. Past about 240 FPS, each upgrade saves under 2 ms per frame. A good monitor and a stable connection may matter more.
