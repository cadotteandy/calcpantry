How gacha pity works

Character banners in Genshin Impact, Honkai: Star Rail and Zenless Zone Zero all use the same system from HoYoverse. Each pull has a small chance of a 5-star (0.6%). After many pulls without one, the chance ramps up quickly (soft pity), and if you reach the 90th pull you're guaranteed a 5-star (hard pity).

When the 5-star arrives, there's a 50% chance it's the featured character. If it isn't, your next 5-star on that banner is guaranteed to be featured. This calculator follows every possible path through those rules and adds up the odds exactly, rather than simulating random pulls.

The model

5-star chance on pull n since the last one:
n ≤ 73: 0.6%
74 ≤ n ≤ 89: 0.6% + 6% × (n − 73)
n = 90: 100%
featured on a 5-star: 50%, or 100% if guaranteed

Saving tips

  • Pity carries over. It doesn't reset between character banners, so pulling a little and stopping at 70 pity sets you up well for the next banner.
  • Know your guarantee. If your last 5-star on the banner wasn't the featured one, your next one is. Tick the box above and your odds jump.
  • Plan for 90%, not 50%. A 50% chance means half of players in your position miss. If you really want a character, save to the 90% line.
