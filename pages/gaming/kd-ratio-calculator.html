How K/D and KDA work

K/D (kill/death ratio) is the simplest skill stat in shooters: how many opponents you take down for every time you go down. KDA adds assists, which credits players who deal damage or set up kills for teammates. MOBAs and hero shooters favour KDA; battle royales and arena shooters usually show K/D.

The formulas

K/D = kills ÷ deaths
KDA = (kills + assists) ÷ deaths
kills needed (no deaths) = target × deaths − kills
deaths until target at match K/D m = (target × deaths − kills) ÷ (m − target)

Raising your K/D

  • Trade smarter, not more. Most deaths come from peeking the same angle twice or rotating in the open. Fewer bad deaths raises K/D faster than more kills.
  • Watch recent stats. Lifetime K/D is slow to move; a weekly or seasonal number shows whether practice is paying off.
  • Get your settings right. A consistent sensitivity and a high, stable frame rate both help aim.
