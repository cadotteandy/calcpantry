How Minecraft storage math works

Every container in Minecraft is measured in slots, and each slot holds one stack. How many items a slot holds depends on the item: 64 for most blocks, 16 for a handful of items, and just 1 for tools, armor and other unstackables. So the first step is always items ÷ stack size, rounded up, to get slots.

The formula

slots = ⌈items ÷ stack size⌉
shulker boxes or chests = ⌈slots ÷ 27⌉
double chests = ⌈slots ÷ 54⌉

Tips for big builds

  • Count in shulker boxes. For mega builds, think in shulkers: 1,728 blocks each. A 100 × 100 floor is 10,000 blocks, about 6 shulker boxes.
  • Ender chests travel with you. Your ender chest holds 27 more slots, and filling it with shulker boxes multiplies what you can carry.
  • Barrels pack tighter. Barrels hold the same 27 slots as a chest but can sit side by side and stack without blocking each other.
