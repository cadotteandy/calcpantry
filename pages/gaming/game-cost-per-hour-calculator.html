Why cost per hour?

A $70 game and a $20 game are hard to compare by price alone. Cost per hour puts them on equal footing: a $70 RPG you play for 150 hours is cheaper per hour than a $20 game you finish in an afternoon. It's also a handy way to decide whether DLC or a season pass is worth it for a game you already play a lot.

The formula

total = price + DLC + (subscription × months) − resale
cost per hour = total ÷ hours played
