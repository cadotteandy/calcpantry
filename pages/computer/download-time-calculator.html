How download time is calculated

Download time is just size divided by speed, once both are in the same units. The catch is that file sizes are measured in bytes and internet speeds in bits. A byte is 8 bits, so a "300 meg" plan moves at most 37.5 megabytes each second.

The formula

time (s) = file size (bytes) × 8 ÷ (speed (bits/s) × efficiency)
MB/s = Mbps ÷ 8

Example: a 100 GB game on 300 Mbps

StepResult
Size in bits: 100 × 10⁹ × 8800 billion bits
Theoretical: 800 × 10⁹ ÷ 300 × 10⁶44 min 27 s

Speeding up big downloads

  • Use Ethernet. A cable is almost always faster and steadier than Wi-Fi, especially for consoles.
  • Pause other traffic. Streaming and cloud backups on other devices share the same connection.
  • Check your drive. On fast fiber, an old hard drive can be slower than the connection while a game installs.
