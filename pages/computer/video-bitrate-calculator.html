How video file size works

Bitrate is how much data a video uses each second. Multiply it by the length and you have the file size. The resolution and frame rate don't appear in the formula directly; they affect how much bitrate you need for the video to look good.

The formula

file size (bytes) = (video bitrate + audio bitrate) × seconds ÷ 8
video bitrate for a target = target size × 8 ÷ seconds − audio bitrate

Example: 10 minutes at 8 Mbps
