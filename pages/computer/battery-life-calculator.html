How battery life is worked out

A battery's real energy content is measured in watt-hours (Wh). Milliamp-hours (mAh) only tell half the story because they depend on voltage: a 3.7 V and a 7.4 V battery with the same mAh hold very different amounts of energy. Convert to watt-hours first, take off conversion losses, then divide by how fast the device uses power.

The formula

Wh = mAh × V ÷ 1000
usable Wh = Wh × efficiency
run time (h) = usable Wh ÷ device watts
charges = usable Wh ÷ device battery Wh

Example: 10,000 mAh power bank running a 5 W device

Energy: 10,000 × 3.7 ÷ 100037 Wh
