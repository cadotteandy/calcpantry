How it's calculated

Electricity is billed by the kilowatt-hour (kWh): 1,000 watts running for one hour. To find what your PC costs, multiply its power draw by the hours it runs, convert to kWh and multiply by your rate. Gaming and idle time are counted separately because a PC sitting at the desktop draws a fraction of its gaming power.

The formula

kWh per day = (gaming W × gaming hours + idle W × idle hours) ÷ 1000
cost per day = kWh per day × rate ($/kWh)
cost per month = cost per day × 30.42

Cutting the cost

  • Cap your frame rate. Limiting FPS to your monitor's refresh rate can cut GPU power substantially with no visible difference.
  • Let it sleep. Set the PC to sleep after 15 to 30 minutes idle instead of leaving it at the desktop.
  • Turn off console instant-on. Energy-saving standby uses far less power than rest modes that keep the console ready.
