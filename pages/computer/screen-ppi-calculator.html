What PPI means

Pixels per inch measures how tightly pixels are packed on a screen. The same resolution looks sharper on a smaller screen because the pixels are smaller and closer together. That's why a 24-inch 1080p monitor looks crisper than a 32-inch one at the same distance.

The formula

diagonal pixels = √(width² + height²)
PPI = diagonal pixels ÷ diagonal inches
width (in) = diagonal × width ÷ diagonal pixels
blend distance (in) ≈ 3438 ÷ PPI
