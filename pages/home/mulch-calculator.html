How the mulch calculator works

Mulch is sold by volume: in bags measured in cubic feet, or in bulk by the cubic yard. To find the volume you need, multiply the bed's area by the depth. Because the area is in square feet and depth is in inches, the depth is divided by 12 first. That gives cubic feet, and dividing by 27 converts to cubic yards (one yard is 3 × 3 × 3 ft).

Bag counts are rounded up, since you can't buy part of a bag. Bulk cost is shown for the exact volume; in practice a landscape supplier will round up to the nearest half or whole yard.

The formula

cubic feet = length × width × (depth in inches ÷ 12)
cubic yards = cubic feet ÷ 27
2 cu ft bags = cubic feet ÷ 2, rounded up
3 cu ft bags = cubic feet ÷ 3, rounded up

Example: a 20 × 10 ft bed at 3 inches

Tips before you order

  • Measure irregular beds in pieces. Break curves into rough rectangles, add the areas, and use the square-feet option. For a round bed, area = 3.14 × radius².
  • Subtract what's already there. If old mulch is still an inch deep, only add the difference.
  • Add a little for settling. Fresh shredded mulch compacts by roughly 10–20% in the first few weeks. Round up rather than down.
  • Keep it off trunks. Pile it 2–3 inches away from tree trunks and stems to avoid rot and pests.
  • Compare delivered prices. For small jobs, a delivery fee can make bulk cost more than bags.
