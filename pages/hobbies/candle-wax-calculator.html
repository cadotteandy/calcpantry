How the candle wax calculation works

Candle wax is measured by weight, but containers are sold by volume, and the "8 oz" on a jar rarely tells you how much wax it takes. The reliable way is to fill the container with water up to where the wax will stop (usually about 1/2 inch below the rim), then weigh that water. Wax is lighter than water, so you multiply the water weight by the wax's specific gravity. Most candle waxes are 0.82 to 0.95 times as dense as water, and 0.86 is the figure most suppliers and calculators use for soy and coconut blends.

That gives the total fill: wax plus fragrance oil. The fragrance load is taken as a share of that total, and the rest is wax. Doing it this way means the finished candle lands exactly at your fill line.

The formula

fill per candle (oz) = water weight (oz) × wax density
fragrance per candle = fill × fragrance load %
wax per candle = fill − fragrance
batch = per candle × number of candles
cost per candle = (wax lb × $/lb + oil oz × $/oz) ÷ candles + jar, wick, label

If you enter fluid ounces instead of a weighed amount, the calculator converts at 1.043 ounces of weight per fluid ounce of water. For a cylinder, it uses π × radius² × fill height, then converts cubic inches to water weight.

These are typical figures. Your wax supplier lists the exact maximum fragrance load for each wax, and that's the number to trust.

Tips for consistent batches

  • Weigh everything. Fragrance oils vary in density, so measure oil on a scale in ounces or grams, not in a measuring cup.
  • Test before scaling up. Pour one or two test candles to check wick size and scent throw. More fragrance isn't always stronger; the wick and wax matter as much.
  • Add fragrance at the right temperature. Most soy waxes call for adding oil around 185°F and stirring for about two minutes to bind it.
  • Plan for a second pour. Soy often sinks around the wick as it cools. Holding back a little melted wax for a top-up is easier than melting a fresh batch.
  • Price for the whole job. A 3–4× materials multiplier leaves room for your time, marketplace fees and breakage.
