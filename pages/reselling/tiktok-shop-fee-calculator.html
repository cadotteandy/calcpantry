How TikTok Shop fees work in 2026

TikTok Shop's main seller charge is the referral fee, a percentage of each completed order. There are no listing fees and no monthly subscription for US sellers.

  • Most categories: 6% in TikTok's published US category table.
  • Select jewelry and pre-owned categories: 5%. Qualifying pre-owned and collectible sales pay 3% on the portion above $10,000.
  • New sellers: 3% for 30 days if the first sale happens within 60 days of finishing onboarding.

A caution on the headline rate: several seller guides report that TikTok raised the rate for most non-food categories to 8% on August 4, 2026, with some beauty and electronics sub-categories higher still, while TikTok's public table continues to show 6%. Your Seller Center and settlement statements are the final word. The calculator includes both rates and a custom option.

The referral fee is charged on what the customer pays, including buyer-paid shipping and platform-funded discounts, minus sales tax. If a creator drove the sale, you also pay the affiliate commission you set, calculated on the product price. For many shops, that commission is a bigger cost than the referral fee.

The formula

referral fee = (product price + shipping buyer pays) × referral rate
affiliate commission = product price × commission rate
payout = product price + shipping buyer pays − referral fee − affiliate commission
profit = payout − product cost − shipping label cost

Example: a $25 product sold through a creator

A creator's video sells your $25 phone stand with free shipping. It costs you $6 to make, the label costs $5, and your affiliate plan pays creators 10%. The category rate is 6%.

Tips for TikTok Shop sellers

  • Set affiliate rates from your margin, not your competitors. Use the break-even price to see how much commission a product can carry before it stops paying.
  • Use the new-seller window. Thirty days at 3% is worth planning a launch around, since the clock starts with your first sale.
  • Plan for refunds. TikTok keeps 20% of the referral fee (up to $5 per item) as a refund administration fee, and you usually lose the shipping label too.
  • Read your settlement statements. Rates vary by category and change often. Compare the fees you were actually charged with this calculator once a month.
  • Count samples and ads. Free samples sent to creators and paid promotion aren't in the referral fee, but they're real costs per order.
