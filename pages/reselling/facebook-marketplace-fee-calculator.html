How Facebook Marketplace fees work in 2026

Facebook Marketplace has two very different cost structures depending on how the item changes hands:

  • Local pickup, paid in person: no fee at all. Facebook doesn't charge to list, and cash or peer-to-peer payments you arrange yourself never pass through Facebook.
  • Shipped orders through Facebook checkout: individual sellers pay a selling fee of 10% per transaction, with a $0.80 minimum.

The fee base is broader than many sellers expect. Meta's seller policy applies the 10% to the entire transaction, including the item price, any shipping the buyer pays, and applicable sales taxes. Items shipped together in one order count as a single transaction, so the minimum applies once per order.

The formula

transaction = (sold price + shipping buyer pays) × (1 + sales tax rate)
selling fee = the greater of transaction × 10% or $0.80
payout = sold price + shipping buyer pays − selling fee
profit = payout − item cost − shipping label cost

Example: a $30 shipped sale

You sell a pair of speakers for $30 with free shipping. You paid $8 for them at a yard sale, and the shipping label costs $7. We'll leave sales tax out to keep the numbers simple.

LineAmount
Selling fee (10% of $30)$3.00
Payout ($30 − $3.00)$27.00
Minus item cost ($8) and label ($7)$12.00 profit
Same sale as local pickup (no fee, no label)$22.00 profit

Shipping costs $10 in this example: $3 in fees plus the $7 label. That's the real trade-off on Marketplace. Local sales keep everything but only reach nearby buyers; shipping opens up the whole country but takes a noticeably bigger bite out of low-priced items.

When the $0.80 minimum kicks in

Any shipped order totaling under $8 pays the $0.80 minimum instead of 10%. On a $5 item, that's an effective rate of 16%, before postage. Once a label is added, most sub-$10 items lose money when shipped, which is why small items work better as local pickups or bundles.

Tips for selling on Facebook Marketplace

  • Offer both options. Listing an item as available for shipping and local pickup widens your audience, and local buyers still pay no fee.
  • Price shipped listings a little higher. If an item goes for $30 locally, you need about $41 shipped to net the same, covering a $7 label and the 10% fee.
  • Weigh before you list. An underestimated label is the most common way a shipped Marketplace sale turns into a loss.
  • Bundle small items. One order pays one minimum fee and one label.
  • Stay on checkout for shipped sales. Facebook's purchase protection and seller protection only cover orders paid through Facebook checkout.
