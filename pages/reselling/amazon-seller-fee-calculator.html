How Amazon seller fees work

Every Amazon sale carries up to four kinds of fees. This calculator adds them up per unit so you can see real profit before you send inventory in or list an item:

  • Selling plan. The Individual plan charges $0.99 per item sold. The Professional plan is a flat $39.99 a month no matter how much you sell, so its cost per unit falls as volume rises. The two cost the same at about 40 units a month.
  • Referral fee. Amazon's commission, a percentage of the total sales price (item price plus any shipping or gift wrap the buyer pays). It's 15% in most categories, 8% for consumer electronics and computers, and tiered in a few categories. Most categories have a $0.30 minimum per item.
  • Closing fee. Books, music, DVDs, video and software add a flat $1.80 per item.
  • FBA fulfillment fee. If Amazon stores and ships your product, it charges a per-unit fee based on size tier and weight. Because those tables change often and depend on exact dimensions, enter the fee from Amazon's Revenue Calculator rather than relying on an estimate.

The formula

total sales price = item price + shipping charged
referral fee = max(total × category rate, $0.30)
Amazon fees = referral fee + plan cost per unit + closing fee + FBA fee
profit = total sales price − Amazon fees − item cost − your shipping & prep
margin = profit ÷ total sales price  ·  ROI = profit ÷ item cost

Tiered categories

Some categories change rate with price. In clothing and accessories the whole price is charged 5% at $15 or less, 10% from $15.01 to $20, and 17% above $20. Beauty, health and baby pay 8% at $10 or less and 15% above; grocery pays 8% at $15 or less and 15% above. Electronics accessories (15% on the first $100, 8% on the rest) and jewelry (20% on the first $250, 5% on the rest) only apply the lower rate to the portion above the threshold. Categories not listed, such as watches, furniture and Amazon device accessories, have their own rates: use the custom option.

Tips for pricing on Amazon

  • Watch the tier edges. A $20.99 shirt pays 17% ($3.57); drop it to $19.99 and it pays 10% ($2.00), so the cheaper price can net you more.
  • Don't forget storage. FBA monthly storage and aged-inventory fees aren't included here. Slow sellers can quietly eat their margin.
  • Price in returns. Categories like clothing see high return rates, and returns cost you shipping and sometimes a refund administration fee.
  • Check the minimum. On very cheap items the $0.30 minimum plus $0.99 per-item fee can take a large share of the sale.
  • Ask about new-seller incentives. Amazon offers credits and fee reductions to new Professional sellers, which can make the first year cheaper than this calculator shows.
