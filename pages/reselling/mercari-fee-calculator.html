How Mercari fees work in 2026

Mercari US charges sellers one fee: a flat 10% selling fee. It applies to the item price plus any shipping the buyer pays. Listing is free, there's no monthly charge, and since January 6, 2025 there's no separate payment processing fee. The old 2.9% + $0.50 charge was dropped when the 10% fee came back.

Buyers pay their own Buyer Protection fee at checkout. That doesn't come out of your earnings, so it isn't part of this calculator.

Buyer-paid vs. seller-paid shipping

This is where most Mercari math goes wrong. When the buyer pays shipping, the label cost is covered at checkout, but your 10% fee is calculated on the item price plus that shipping. When you offer free shipping, the fee applies to the item price only, and the full label cost is deducted from your earnings. Free shipping usually sells faster, so many sellers build the label into the price.

Older listings

Listings created before January 6, 2025 and never edited since stay on the previous structure. On those, the seller pays no selling fee and the buyer pays a 2.9% + $0.50 processing fee. Any edit moves the listing to the current 10% fee. Tick the box in the calculator if this applies to you.

Getting paid

Direct deposit to your bank is free. Instant Pay sends your balance to a debit card in minutes for a flat $3 per transfer. Because the fee is per transfer, not per sale, cash out several sales at once if you use it.

The formula

selling fee = (sold price + buyer-paid shipping) × 10%
payout = sold price − selling fee − seller-paid label − Instant Pay fee
profit = payout − item cost

Tips for Mercari sellers

  • Compare shipping options before you list. Mercari's prepaid labels are priced by weight; weigh the packed item so you don't pick a tier that's too light.
  • Price for offers. Buyers expect to negotiate. Set your floor at the break-even price plus the profit you want.
  • Batch your Instant Pay transfers. $3 on a $15 sale is 20% of the sale; spread over five sales it barely registers.
  • Relist carefully. Editing an old, never-updated listing moves it to the 10% fee.
  • Track your cost basis. You'll need it at tax time if Mercari sends a 1099-K.
