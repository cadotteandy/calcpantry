How Etsy fees work in 2026

Etsy stacks several small fees rather than charging one big one. For a US shop selling to a US buyer, every order pays three:

  • Listing fee: $0.20 per listing. It lasts four months or until the item sells. With auto-renew on, Etsy charges another $0.20 each time a multi-quantity listing sells.
  • Transaction fee: 6.5% of the item price plus the shipping and gift wrap you charge. It is not charged on sales tax.
  • Payment processing: 3% + $0.25 per order through Etsy Payments. This one is calculated on the full order total, including sales tax.

Some orders carry a fourth. If a buyer clicks one of Etsy's ads on another site (Google, Instagram, Pinterest and others) and buys within 30 days, you pay an Offsite Ads fee of 15% of the order total. Shops with over $10,000 in sales in the past 12 months pay 12% and can't opt out. The fee is capped at $100 per order.

The formula

sale = item price + shipping charged + gift wrap
transaction fee = sale × 6.5%
processing fee = sale × (1 + sales tax rate) × 3% + $0.25
Etsy fees = $0.20 + transaction fee + processing fee + Offsite Ads fee
profit = sale − Etsy fees − materials − shipping label

Example: a $32 order with $6 shipping

You sell a handmade candle for $32 and charge $6 shipping. Materials cost $8 and the label costs $6. No Offsite Ad was involved, and we'll leave sales tax out to keep the numbers clean.

If that same order came through an Offsite Ad at 15%, add $5.70 in fees and profit drops to $14.24. That's the single biggest swing in Etsy pricing, so it's worth knowing whether your shop is enrolled.

Pricing tips for Etsy sellers

  • Charge for your time. Materials are only part of the cost. Put your hourly labor into the item cost field to see whether the price actually pays you.
  • Plan for Offsite Ads. If you're over $10,000 a year, a share of your orders will carry the 12% fee. Price so those orders still make money.
  • Free shipping doesn't avoid fees. Etsy charges the same percentages whether shipping is a separate line or built into the price.
  • Buy labels through Etsy. Etsy's discounted labels are often cheaper than retail postage, which lowers your label cost without touching the buyer's price.
  • Watch small orders. The fixed $0.45 ($0.20 listing + $0.25 processing) is 9% of a $5 sale before any percentage fees.
