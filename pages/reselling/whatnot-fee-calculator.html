How Whatnot fees work in 2026

Whatnot charges two fees on every sale, whether it happens in a live show, the marketplace or your shop:

  • Commission: 8% of the item's sale price in most US categories. Shipping and sales tax are not included.
  • Payment processing: 2.9% + $0.30 per order, calculated on the total the buyer pays, which does include shipping and tax.

There are no listing fees or subscriptions. Buyers normally pay shipping at checkout, and Whatnot uses that money to generate your label, so shipping only costs you something when you offer free or discounted shipping.

The new volume tiers

Since September 21, 2026, Whatnot sets your commission from your sales over a four-week period. Sellers under $15,000 in that window pay the standard 8%. Above that, rates step down by category, reaching 6.5% for sports and trading cards, 5% for other collectibles, 4.5% for fashion and 4% for everything else at $500,000. If you've unlocked a lower tier, choose "Custom" and enter the rate from your dashboard.

In select categories, Whatnot also charges 0% commission on the part of a single sale above $1,500. Processing still applies to the whole order.

The formula

commission = sold price × 8%
processing = (sold price + shipping buyer pays) × (1 + sales tax rate) × 2.9% + $0.30
Whatnot fees = commission + processing
profit = sold price − Whatnot fees − shipping you cover − item cost

Example: a $50 sale with $8 shipping

A trading card you bought for $15 sells for $50 in a live show. The buyer pays $8 shipping, which covers the label, and we'll leave sales tax out to keep the numbers clean.

Notice that the $8 shipping still adds about $0.23 to the processing fee even though you never keep it. Sales tax works the same way: at 8% tax, the processing fee on this order rises by about $0.13.

Tips for Whatnot sellers

  • Know your auction floor. The break-even price above is the lowest a starting bid or buy-it-now price can go before a sale loses money.
  • Watch $1 starts. A $1 auction still pays $0.30 plus processing on the shipping, so low-end sales can end up costing you money after the item's cost.
  • Price in giveaways and free shipping. Anything you cover to build a show comes out of your margin. Add it to "Shipping you cover" or the item cost.
  • Track your four-week sales. Crossing $15,000 lowers your commission for the next period, which can be worth timing a big show around.
  • Check your statements. In some states Whatnot must charge tax on its commission and fees, which adds a little on top of the numbers here.
