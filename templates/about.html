About {{ site.name }}

{{ site.name }} is a collection of free calculators for everyday decisions: what you'll earn selling online, how much material a project needs, how much to feed a pet, and what a side gig really pays.

Every calculator runs in your browser. Nothing you type is sent to us or stored. Each page explains the formula it uses, so you can check the math yourself.

Fees, tax rates and product specs change. We review figures regularly and note when each page was last checked. If you spot something out of date, please let us know.
