About {{ site.name }}

{{ site.name }} is a free collection of calculators for the small, practical questions that come up every day: what you'll actually take home from a sale, how many bags of mulch a bed needs, how much to feed a puppy, how long a download will take or how much water goes with a cup of rice.

It's run by Bailey, an independent designer and creator. The idea is simple: each page should answer one question quickly, work well on a phone, and show its working so you never have to take a number on faith.

What makes these calculators different

  • The math is on the page. Every calculator explains the formula it uses and walks through a worked example with real numbers, so you can check the result yourself.
  • Figures come from the source. Marketplace fees, tax rates, food-safety temperatures and scientific constants are taken from the organizations that publish them, and those pages list their sources.
  • Every page shows when it was last checked. Fees and rates change, so each calculator notes the month its figures were reviewed.
  • Nothing you type leaves your device. The calculations run in your browser. There are no accounts and no sign-ups.

Read more about how pages are written, tested and kept up to date in How we check our calculators.
