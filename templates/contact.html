Reporting a correction

To get a fix in quickly, please include:

  • the page address (or the calculator's name),
  • the numbers you entered and the result you got,
  • what you expected, and a link to the official figure if a rate or fee has changed.

Corrections are checked against the original source and the page's "last checked" date is updated once fixed. You can read more about that process in How we check our calculators.

Suggestions

Ideas for new calculators are always welcome. Tell us what you were trying to work out and what numbers you had to start with.
